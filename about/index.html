Bio

Itai is an illustrator and graphic designer based in Tel Aviv.

Trained in both visual communication and history studies, his work is driven by curiosity to the world around him: its systems, symbols, and the quiet logic holding it together.

Itai's visual language is expressive, witty, and emotionally direct. It draws inspiration from the symbolic gestures of medieval illustration as much as from the reductive logic of mid-century modernist graphics.
